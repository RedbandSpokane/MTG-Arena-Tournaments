Magic: The Gathering Arena

Weekly Tournament

All times are Pacific Time.

How it works

Swiss style. Nobody gets knocked out. Each round you play someone with the same record as you, and the best record at the end of the week wins.

A new tournament starts every week. The organizer picks round robin (everyone plays everyone) or Swiss (play people with the same record) depending on how many players sign up.

Best of three. Win two games to win the match. A match win is worth 3 points and a draw is worth 1.

Each week

  1. Sign in on My matches with your name and the 4-digit PIN the organizer gave you.
  2. Tap the hours you're free that week, then save. The more hours you mark, the easier it is to find a time.
  3. When the organizer posts matches, you'll see each opponent and a few suggested times you're both free. Pick one. Once your opponent accepts, it's on the schedule.
  4. Play your match any time during the week, or with the group during the Saturday session.
  5. After the match, either player reports the result.

Playing on Arena

Add your Arena username on My matches so your opponent can find you. At match time, add each other as friends in MTG Arena and send a challenge set to Best of 3.

Arena is free on phones, tablets, and computers.
